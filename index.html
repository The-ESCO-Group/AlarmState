NEW TECHNICIAN ORIENTATION — PGP PLANT 7

Your job

You are the on-shift I&C technician. When an alarm comes in, find the root cause and identify it by the tagname of the I/O point that is causing the problem (e.g. TCV-2208). Type it into the SOLVE PROBLEM box (ENTER key) before the plant shuts down.

Alarm escalation — 2 minutes per tier

TIER 1 — WARNING → TIER 2 — CRITICAL → TIER 3 — PLANT SHUTDOWN

Solve in Tier 1 for maximum points. A shutdown is a strike — three strikes and corporate ends your shift. During a shutdown you have 2 more minutes to identify the cause for partial credit before the round is lost.

Your tools

  • HMI terminals (E) — FactoryTalk-style operator screens in the control room and at field panels. Live process values, equipment status, alarm summary and trends. The HMI jumps straight to the screen for the active alarm.
  • Engineering workstation (E) — the development node in the control room. Opens the ladder logic for the routine that generated the alarm so you aren't digging through the whole program. Click any tagname to cross-reference it.
  • Radio (R) — key up and ask operators and supervisors questions. They answer based on what they can see. Some of them are wrong. Some of them are jerks. On GRAVEYARD, at least one of them is actively feeding you garbage.
  • Walk the plant (WASD) — listen for steam leaks, look for stopped motors, beacons, and leaks. The plant itself is a clue.

Scoring

  • Solve in WARNING: 1000 pts + time bonus · CRITICAL: 600 + time bonus · during SHUTDOWN: 200
  • Wrong guess: −100 pts (and the supervisor will hear about it)
  • Difficulty multiplier: Day x1 · Swing x1.5 · Graveyard x2 · Streak bonus up to +50%

Reading tags

ISA style: first letter = variable (T temp, P pressure, L level, F flow, A analytic, I current, S speed, Z position), then modifiers (T transmitter, CV control valve, SH/SL switch high/low). Number = area: 11xx prep, 22xx extraction, 33xx refinery, 41xx boiler, 42xx cooling, 51xx meal.
