Your assignment

You are T. Ryder, Plant 7's probationary controls technician. The plant failed an alarm-management audit. Shift Supervisor Frank Kowalski controls your work orders; Plant Manager Dana Voss controls whether you remain in the program. Every delay, unsupported answer, escalation, and shutdown is recorded.

The campaign loop

Follow the amber route immediately, take the assigned work, prove the required result, and report for debrief. Management will interrupt when you waste time. The qualification log records all 21 assignments and does not erase poor response history.

ESCO Automation Studio

Programming assignments open only from the marked ESCO workstation and remain locked until your actual project passes the requirement. Live production calls enable the prebuilt HMI with H and controller diagnostics with Tab. The brief gives context and one conceptual hint, never a completed rung or HMI answer.

Live fault calls

Travel to the affected equipment, compare field evidence with HMI values, logic, trends, and radio reports, then press Enter and submit the root-cause I/O tag such as TCV-2208.

Alarm escalation

TIER 1 — WARNING → TIER 2 — CRITICAL → TIER 3 — PLANT SHUTDOWN

Escalation windows are 95 seconds on early calls, 75 seconds on normal calls, and 55 seconds on hard calls. A shutdown is a strike and still requires an incident cause. Three strikes terminate the shift and send the failed assignment back to dispatch.
